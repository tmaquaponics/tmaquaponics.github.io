### Overview

In my [fishroom in a rack build](https://tmaquaponics.github.io/2026/04/03/Building-nano-RAS.html){:target="_blank"}, the cornerstone piece of the design is a sump. It serves the role of a settling chamber and a central filtration system. In this post, I will discuss the different designs I drew and consider the pros and cons of each model. Each design is imagined from first principles. Finally, I will document how I built the design I went with and the difficulties I encountered. Though the one I chose is simple, it serves its purpose quite well and offers me plenty of space and possibilities for upgrade and modifications.
